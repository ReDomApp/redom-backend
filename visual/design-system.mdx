## Interface patterns

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Navigation">
    - Keep primary navigation predictable.
    - Show the active destination clearly.
    - Preserve a compact hierarchy on mobile.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cards">
    - Use cards to group related tasks or information.
    - Keep titles descriptive and metadata secondary.
    - Avoid unnecessary decoration and excessive nesting.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Forms">
    - Keep labels visible and validation close to the field.
    - Distinguish idle, loading, error, success, and disabled states.
    - Do not represent a pending payment as a completed payment.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Asset rules

- Prefer the actual SVG and image assets in the product's own asset directories.
- Do not redraw or replace a supplied brand mark when the original asset is available.
- Keep product-specific palettes distinct where a product has its own documented theme.
- Treat this page's shell illustration as conceptual until checked against the running app.
