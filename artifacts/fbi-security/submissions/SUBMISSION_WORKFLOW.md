# Official Submission Workflow

1. Open the case's official source from the allowlisted source registry.
2. Reconfirm that the case is active and the reward terms have not changed.
3. Open the official reporting destination from the registry or case page. Validate the exact host and HTTPS URL; do not follow reporting links from social media or suspicious messages.
4. Review every report claim against its cited source. Label unverified information and uncertainty clearly.
5. Remove unnecessary sensitive personal data and any data obtained without authorization.
6. Generate a draft report. This project must not auto-submit or auto-email the report.
7. A human reviews the content and manually submits it through the authority's official workflow.
8. Record only a minimal submission audit event (case ID, timestamp, channel, and status). Do not store copies of government confirmation messages or personal data unless necessary and authorized.
9. Reward eligibility and claim processes are determined by the responsible agency and the exact program terms; never promise a payment.

## FBI

- Public wanted list: https://www.fbi.gov/wanted
- Electronic tip portal: https://tips.fbi.gov/

Always use the case-specific reporting instructions where provided.

## Other agencies

Use the agency's official source registry and current instructions. A press release or enforcement action does not automatically imply there is a reward. The SEC whistleblower program, criminal tip lines, consumer fraud portals, and fugitive reward offers have different requirements.
