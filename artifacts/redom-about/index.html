What ReDom is

ReDom is designed around people and their everyday digital interactions: connecting with others, sharing content, communicating, discovering experiences, participating in marketplace activity and using ReDom Payments where supported.

Official policies, security information, payment documentation and user guidance are maintained through the ReDom Documentation and Help Center.

Payments

ReDom Payments can present payment methods according to account, customer, country, currency, transaction and payment-method eligibility. The current payment configuration includes cards and a range of wallets, bank and local payment methods.
